About Erachap

Erachap started with one idea: furniture doesn't have to whisper. Every piece we make leans into color, shape, and material instead of hiding from it — molded plastic in shades you won't find in a showroom catalog, forms borrowed from the Memphis design movement of the 80s and pushed further.

We design and produce in Yerevan, working directly with local workshops to keep every piece affordable without cutting the personality out of it. This isn't furniture that matches your room. It's furniture your room adjusts to.
